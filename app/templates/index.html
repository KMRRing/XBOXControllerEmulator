What the game sees

Layout

Idle controls below 100 % is the ghost mode: a control fades to that much of the opacity until a finger is on it, 0 % being invisible. Floating sticks centre wherever the thumb lands. The trackpad, the mouse buttons and the keys K1–K4 are off until you switch them on below: on the trackpad, drag moves the cursor, tap clicks, two fingers tap for a right click, tap and go straight back down to drag, two fingers up and down scroll.

Drag a control to move it; arrow keys nudge it one grid step, Shift for four. With Mirror on, the right side follows the left: LT/RT, LB/RB, Back/Start and the stick clicks match in place and size; A B X Y move and size as one cluster whose centre mirrors the left stick; the right stick mirrors the D-pad.
